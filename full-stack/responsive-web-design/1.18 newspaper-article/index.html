25th March 1992

Ai giberish makes little girl cry

Annabelle asks for help from an Ai agent, the agen hurts her feelings...

By Adiuka

What a twist of fate, we build technology to help people, but we never consider the dangers it might bring. Annabelle, a joung girl who is still in primary school, asked an agent (who shall not be named), how to end all of the world wars she is hearing about.

The prompt failed, and in a tragic twist of fate answered that there is no way, asside from conquering the enemies of her country. It then continued to state that world peace may not be attainable in her life time, and that there is nothing, she as a normal every day person from a small town in missisippi, can do to stop that.
