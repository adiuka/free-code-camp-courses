Ozzy Osbourne

The prince of Darkness

Image of Ozzy Osbourne ir yellow Matador's shirt shouting like hell. He also has blonde hair.

He is known as the “Prince of Darkness,” with a voice that became one of the defining sounds of heavy metal. As the frontman of Black Sabbath, he helped shape and popularize the genre in its earliest days. His stage presence is eerie and theatrical, often paired with famously wild and controversial antics. His distinct, nasal singing style makes him instantly recognizable across generations of rock fans. Behind the persona, his life has been marked by chaos, excess, and resilience. Later on, he reintroduced himself to a new audience as a surprisingly humorous and relatable TV personality.
